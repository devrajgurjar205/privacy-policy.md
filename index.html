# Privacy Policy for Byaj Calculator Last Updated: October 2026 Byaj Calculator respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use the app. 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT Byaj Calculator is designed to work primarily as an offline interest calculator. - The application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information (such as name, email, or phone number). - The calculation data entered by the user (principal amount, duration, and monthly interest rate) is processed locally on the device only. 2. CALCULATION DATA & LOCAL STORAGE - The amounts and values entered into the calculator are used solely to compute interest and total amounts. - If Calculation History is enabled, history data is stored locally on the user's device using an internal database. - The application does not upload or transmit calculation history to any external server. 3. INTERNET CONNECTION - Core calculator features work 100% offline without an active internet connection. - Internet connectivity is only used for third-party advertising services (Google AdMob). 4. ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB) - Byaj Calculator may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. - AdMob may collect and process device-related identifiers and telemetry to serve and measure advertisements in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads 5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES - Google Play Services (https://policies.google.com/privacy) - Google AdMob (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) 6. DATA SECURITY We employ standard practices to ensure locally stored data remains secure on your device. 7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY Byaj Calculator is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information. 8. DATA DELETION Users can delete any saved calculation or clear the entire history at any time using the in-app "Clear History" button. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data. 9.
